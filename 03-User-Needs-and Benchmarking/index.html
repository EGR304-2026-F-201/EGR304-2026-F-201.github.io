    • Vendor: Home Depot.

    • Description: A cabinet with 64 drawers for small parts. This benchmark relates to PartPort's component storage and organization.

    Review Sources:

    • Home Depot customer reviews
    • Additional Home Depot reviews
    • eBay reviews of the same model

    Customer comments below are paraphrased. Explicit needs are directly expressed in the feedback. Latent needs are inferred for PartPort.

    Positive Comments
    Voice of the Customer Restated Customer Need
    rllhomestead — eBay, 4 stars, November 26, 2020: Personal numbering labels made stored supplies easy to find. Components are easy to locate. (explicit)
    Users can apply their own identification system. (explicit)
    vault42-ri — eBay, 5 stars, March 24, 2018: Drawers stayed open, moved to the workbench, and returned easily. Selected components can be brought to the workspace. (explicit)
    Storage remains open during loading and retrieval. (explicit)
    Users can easily return filled storage containers. (explicit)
    paugraue0 — eBay, 5 stars, November 14, 2017: Praised retained drawers, generous capacity, stacking, and wall mounting. Containers remain secured during normal access. (explicit)
    Each location holds useful component quantities. (explicit)
    Capacity can expand with the collection. (latent)
    Storage leaves workbench space available. (latent)
    Goldie — Home Depot, 4 stars, December 15, 2015: The cabinet arrived assembled and offered good value. Initial setup requires little assembly. (explicit)
    The purchase price is affordable. (explicit)
    Ron — Home Depot, 5 stars, August 4, 2015: Praised drop resistance and editable markings; some markings were obscured. Storage withstands accidental drops. (explicit)
    Users can update component identification. (explicit)
    Identification is readable at every location. (explicit)
    JDeer — Home Depot, 4 stars, January 3, 2024: Found the cabinet useful, but frame clearance reduced usable dimensions. Usable internal dimensions are clearly stated. (explicit)

    Voice of the Customer Restated Customer Need

    HomeDepotCustomer — Home Depot, 5 stars, August 5, 2026: Said the bin is the perfect size for items you want to keep together but don't have a lot of. Storage size matches small component quantities. (explicit)
    JEFFREY A — Home Depot, 5 stars, June 17, 2026: Said the bins are nice to have on the workbench for quick-pick items. Components can be quickly retrieved during use. (explicit)

    RENE — Home Depot, 4 stars, August 13, 2025: Said the product looked much bigger in the photo than its actual size; didn't have a tape measure at time of ordering and later returned it without any issues. Product photos accurately represent actual size. (explicit)
    Returns can be completed without complications. (explicit)
