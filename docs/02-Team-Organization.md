## Team Charter

After discussing our shared academic, professional, and project goals, the team agreed on the following charter:

> Our team will communicate, stay organized, and make sure everyone contributes to the project. We want to meet deadlines, produce quality work, and build skills that will help us as engineers


Detailed team goals and planning can be reviewed in the [Appendix: Team Organization](https://EGR304-2026-F-201.github.io/Appendix/App-Team-Org/).


## Product Mission Statement

The team developed the following mission based on the intended users and purpose of our product:

 > Our product's mission is to create a useful and reliable embedded system that solves a real-world problem. We want it to be practical, easy to use, and something that shows what we learned in the course.
