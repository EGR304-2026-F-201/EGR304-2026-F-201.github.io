## Team Charter

After discussing our shared academic, professional, and project goals, the team agreed on the following charter:

> We will work collaboratively to design, build, test, and document a reliable embedded system that addresses a meaningful real world need. Each member will contribute by completing assigned work on time, communicating progress and challenges, taking initiative, supporting teammates, and maintaining high technical and ethical standards. We will make decisions respectfully, hold one another accountable, and use feedback to continuously improve both our product and our teamwork.



Detailed team goals and planning can be reviewed in the [Appendix: Team Organization](https://EGR304-2026-F-201.github.io/Appendix/App-Team-Org/).


## Product Mission Statement

The team developed the following mission based on the intended users and purpose of our product:

 > Our mission is to provide engineering students and technicians with a compact, reliable, and easy-to-use component organization system. By automatically rotating the selected component compartment into position, PartPort will reduce the time spent searching for small parts and help users maintain an organized workspace.
