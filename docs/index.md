## About Fill-A-Bot

Fill-A-Bot is a device that dispenses liquid into a user's container of choice based on inputs put in by the user. This device is for the effeciency-minded cook by providing consistent pouring of measured liquids with minimal cleanup. This system makes use of 4 microcontroller-enabled subsystem boards that ensure smooth and consistent operation. The process that has gotten us to where we are currently is extensive and has consisted of numerous brainstorm sessions, discussions regarding exact functionality, and reviews of each other's designs. This project has required us to take accountability for our own subtasks and has given us opportunities to learn in an engineering team setting. While Fill-A-Bot may not be a real product, what we've learned while developing it certainly is real.
